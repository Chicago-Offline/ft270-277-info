## Header comparison with the FT-270R

```
FT-270R  41 48 30 32 32 24 00 01 00 81 47 33 02 00 00 00   AH022$....G3....
FT-277R  41 48 30 32 32 55 80 01 03 04 62 55 24 00 00 00   AH022U....bU$...
```

The model ID differs at byte 5 (`$` = 0x24 vs `U` = 0x55), exactly as CHIRP's
`VX170Radio` / `VX177Radio` pair expects. Bytes 6+ differ too — those are
radio-specific header/config, not part of the 6-byte model field.

## Contents

Near-factory: **1 of 200 channels occupied**, 1277 of 6057 bytes non-zero.

Channel 1 is `462.5500 +duplex`, named `FAMRPT` — a GMRS repeater pair, inside
the driver's 420–470 MHz range but outside the 70 cm amateur allocation. Noted
as an observation about this radio's current contents, not a recommendation;
GMRS requires a GMRS licence and type-accepted equipment.

Useful as a **baseline** for diffing future reads.
